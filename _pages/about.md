<p style="text-align: left;">
Welcome! I am a fourth-year Ph.D. candidate in Economics, enrolled in a joint program between the <a href="https://www.unicatt.it/en.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Catholic University of Milan</a> in Italy and <a href="https://welcome.pantheonsorbonne.fr/en/about" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University</a> in France. I am conducting my doctoral work under the supervision of <a href="https://sites.google.com/site/montobbiofabio/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Fabio Montobbio</a> and <a href="https://lorenzocassi.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Prof. Lorenzo Cassi</a>. In parallel, I am working as an Associate Statistical Researcher at the <a href="https://www.unido.org/about-us/who-we-are" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">United Nations Industrial Development Organization (UNIDO)</a>.
</p>

<p style="text-align: left;">
My research mostly focuses on applied macroeconomic development, studying the extent to which structural change and manufacturing remain engines of long-term growth. This interest has also led me to investigate the diverging patterns of industrialisation and deindustrialisation that have unfolded since the mid-twentieth century. To address these questions, I reconstruct cross-country historical series at the two-digit industrial level to assess whether manufacturing activities matter equally for development and whether they differ in their exposure to the major changes underway since the 1980s.  Broadly, my work aims to shed light on the sectoral levers countries can rely on to promote convergence.
</p>

<p style="text-align: left;">
As part of my Ph.D., I have taken up visiting positions at the <a href="https://commerce.uct.ac.za/prism/about" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">University of Cape Town (UCT)</a> in South Africa and at two institutes of the <a href="https://unu.edu/about" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">United Nations University (UNU)</a> — <a href="https://unu.edu/merit/about-unu-merit" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UNU-MERIT</a> in the Netherlands and <a href="https://www.wider.unu.edu/about" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UNU-WIDER</a> in Finland. I am also a Non-Tenure Teaching and Research Fellow (ATER) at Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University for the 2026–2027 academic year.
</p>
